# Hospital Appointment No-Show Analysis


## Executive Summary

This project focused on understanding why patients miss their hospital appointments and identifying factors that influence attendance behavior. The dataset consisted of 110,527 appointment records and 62,299 unique patients. Using Python, MySQL, and Power BI, I explored patient demographics, waiting times, appointment schedules, health conditions, and reminder systems to uncover meaningful patterns.

## Key Findings

### 1. Waiting Time Has a Strong Impact on Attendance

Patients who had to wait longer for their appointments were more likely to miss them. As waiting days increased, the no-show rate also increased, indicating that long delays between scheduling and appointment dates negatively affect patient commitment.

**Business Impact:** Reducing waiting periods may help improve attendance rates and reduce appointment losses.

---

### 2. Attendance Varies Across Different Weekdays

The analysis showed that some weekdays experienced higher no-show rates than others. This suggests that appointment attendance is influenced by scheduling patterns and patient availability.

**Business Impact:** Hospitals can improve resource planning by identifying high-risk days and implementing additional reminder strategies.

---

### 3. Age Groups Show Different Attendance Patterns

Attendance behavior differed across age groups. Certain age segments were more likely to miss appointments, while others demonstrated more consistent attendance.

**Business Impact:** Patient engagement strategies can be tailored to age groups that have a higher likelihood of missing appointments.

---

### 4. SMS Reminders Influence Patient Behavior

Patients who received SMS reminders showed different attendance trends compared to those who did not receive reminders. This highlights the importance of communication in reducing missed appointments.

**Business Impact:** Expanding automated reminder systems can help hospitals improve attendance and reduce operational inefficiencies.

---

### 5. Geographic Differences Exist Among Patients

Several neighborhoods consistently recorded higher no-show rates than others. These differences suggest that location-specific factors may influence attendance behavior.

**Business Impact:** Targeted outreach programs can be implemented in high-risk neighborhoods to improve appointment adherence.

---

### 6. Health Conditions Affect Appointment Attendance

Patients with chronic health conditions such as hypertension and diabetes displayed attendance patterns that differed from the general patient population.

**Business Impact:** Understanding the needs of patients with chronic conditions can help healthcare providers design better follow-up and care management strategies.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. Reduce appointment waiting times where possible.
2. Increase the use of SMS reminder systems.
3. Monitor attendance performance by weekday.
4. Develop targeted engagement programs for high-risk patient groups.
5. Focus outreach efforts on neighborhoods with consistently high no-show rates.
6. Continuously track attendance KPIs through dashboards and reporting systems.

## Conclusion

The analysis revealed that patient attendance is influenced by multiple factors, including waiting time, age, appointment scheduling patterns, communication methods, and geographic location. By addressing these factors, healthcare organizations can reduce no-show rates, improve operational efficiency, and enhance patient care outcomes.
